From the three missions, I have learned that turning a mode's response into useful robot behavior is much more difficult than I had imaged. Even giving clearer requests did not achieve what I wanted. In Mission 1, the supplied request got a got a better response than mine. It gave a plan whereas my prompt only returned "I'm ready to assist". In mission 2, the model answered "tape measure" in all the runs even though it is supposed to be a drafting compass. In mission 3, my prompt had 32 moves, 16 blocked actions, and 2 STOP but only stayed in column 1 even though the supplied "map" prompt covered 13 cells and got to column 7. One limitation that remains is how the model seems to keep on repeating its old hallucinations and doesn't seem to learn or give different answers even when the prompt says to do it differently.
